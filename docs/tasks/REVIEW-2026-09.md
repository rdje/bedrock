## Goal

Close every finding of the consolidated review at
`docs/reviews/2026-09-30-consolidated-review.md` (items `BK-xx`, `BR-xx`, `NT-xx`), under the
three decisions recorded the same day: [`decision_neutral_spine_and_packs`](../decisions/decision_neutral_spine_and_packs.md),
[`decision_ownership_contract`](../decisions/decision_ownership_contract.md) and
[`decision_licence`](../decisions/decision_licence.md). The exit criterion of the tree is the
review's §9 test matrix: every RED case refused with its expected reason, every GREEN control
passing, in a conformance suite that CI runs.

## Non-Goals

- Rewriting history. The replay of the new contract over past commits (BK-02) is recorded, not
  used to amend commits.
- Repository settings on the hosting platform (branch protection, required checks, the template
  flag). They are documented with the exact settings a child must enable; they cannot be a file.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each leaf below lands with the review IDs it covers named, its own evidence, and the
  conformance suite green.
- The suite reproduces the review's §10 scenarios and the §9 additions, and refuses each RED
  case for the stated reason.
- `DOCTRINE_VERSION` is bumped per leaf and `CHANGELOG.md` carries a migration note where a
  child has to act.
